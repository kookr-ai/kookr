## Hashtags

Skip hashtags on X. They visibly age a post and rarely add reach on dev-tools content; the algorithm prefers reply velocity over tag density.

## Hook line analysis

Both variants open with the same pivot: a specific behaviour ("five agents in parallel") followed by an unexpected cost ("more time switching"). X readers scroll twice as fast as LinkedIn readers — the hook must land in <1 second of reading.

## Posting time

For the dev audience on X, recommend Tuesday or Thursday, 8–10am US Eastern (≈ 2–4pm Paris) — that window catches the US morning + EU afternoon overlap with the highest dev-tools engagement.

## Cross-post protocol

If both Variant B (thread) and the LinkedIn post go up the same day, stagger by ≥2 hours so people who follow on both platforms don't get the exact same wording twice in a row. X first, then LinkedIn 2h later, is the recommended order — X discussions feed back into LinkedIn comments better than the reverse.
