## Posting guidance

- **Tuesday or Wednesday, ~3–5pm Europe/Paris.** Catches US morning when dev-Twitter is most active.
- Single tweet first. If it crosses 100 likes in the first 2 hours, post the thread as a reply chain quoting the original (extends reach without spamming followers).
- Reply to your own first tweet with the demo release URL and the install one-liner (mirroring the LinkedIn pattern of keeping `git clone` out of the main body to dodge the spam classifier).

## Why no "founder thread"

The video is the asset. A 12-tweet "building in public" thread reads as desperate for engagement and dilutes the falsifiable hook. If the post does well, follow up a week later with the *measurement methodology* (how I counted terminal switches) as a separate post — that's a thread Twitter will share because it's a methods-claim, not a marketing pitch.
